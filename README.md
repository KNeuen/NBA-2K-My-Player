# **Player Progression System** 

## **Single Game Achievements** 

_Do not simcast when already loaded into a game_ 

### **Scoring** 

- **0.1 points per point scored** 

   - _ie 10 points = 1 progression point_ 

### **Rebounding** 

- **0.25 points per rebound secured** 

   - _ie 5 rebounds = 1.25 progression point_ 

### **Assists** 

- **0.2 points per assist secured** 

   - _ie 5 assists = 1 progression point_ 

### **Defense** 

- **Each Steal: 1 point** 

- **Each Block: 1 point** 

### **Defensive Assignments** 

- **Holding Main Defensive Assignment to:** 

   - **Under 40% FG Percentage (minimum 10 attempts): 2 points** 

   - **– Below Opponent’s Season Average Points: 1 point** 

### **Performance Bonuses** 

- **Double-Double (excluding turnovers): 3 points** 

- **Triple-Double (excluding turnovers): 7 points** 

- **Quadruple-Double (excluding turnovers): 15 points** 

- **Player of the Game: 1 point** 

- **Perfect Shooting Game (FG% 100% with minimum 7 attempts): 3 points** 

1 

### **Additional Bonuses** 

### **Team Play** 

- **Every 15 Team Assists: 1 point** 

### **Shooting Efficiency** 

- **Field Goal Percentage** _≥_ **50% (min 10 attempts): 2 points** 

- **Three-Point Percentage** _≥_ **40% (min 5 attempts): 2 points** 

### **Milestone Achievements** 

- **First Career Double-Double: 5 points** 

- **First Career Triple-Double: 10 points** 

- **Quadruple-Double: 15 points** 

### **Penalties** 

- **Each Turnover: -0.25 points** 

- **Every 4 Turnovers: Additional -2 points** 

- **Shooting Under 35% FG with More Than 12 Attempts: -3 points** 

- **Outscored by Defensive Assignment by 10+ Points: -2 points** 

- **Fouling Out: -3 points** 

- **Lose the Game: -0.5 points**

## **Badge Costs** 

_Badge costs are cumulative: ie it will cost 15 points to upgrade a badge from no rank to gold._ 

- **Bronze Badge: 3 points** 

- **Silver Badge: 5 points** 

- **Gold Badge: 7 points** 

- **Hall of Fame Badge: 10 points** 

- **Legend Badge: 15 points**
